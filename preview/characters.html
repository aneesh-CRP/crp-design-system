Where these came from: the Meet our Studies in Philadelphia and Meet our Studies in Pennington recruitment sheets. One character per indication — reuse the existing character rather than commissioning a new one.
  • Use for participant-facing recruitment: study sheets, social posts, event handouts, postcards.
  • Do not use in sponsor or investigator materials, the ops dashboard, or anything clinical — the register is warm and informal, which reads wrong to a monitor.
  • Never pair a character with a specific outcome claim. They illustrate the condition, never the investigational product or its effect.
  • Place on white or Cyan #A2DCEB bands, as on the source sheets. All files are transparent PNGs — no background plate needed.
  • Style is outside the April-2023 brand guide (that guide predates them). Treat this card as the governing reference until the guide is revised.
