What made it work

  • Lp(a) testing in house. We settled the eligibility criterion before screening rather than during it.
  • Cardiology referral base. The practices sending us ASCVD patients are the same ones we have worked with for years.
  • One coordinator queue. Every identified patient has a single owner, so nobody is worked twice or dropped.
  • Both sites eligible. Philadelphia and Pennington drew from separate catchments on the same protocol.

Why the screen-fail rate is not a problem

  • The 80% screen-fail rate is normal for an Lp(a) outcomes trial. We absorbed it rather than passing it back as a recruitment problem.
